PDF Merge and Split - 隐私政策 (中文)

应用信息

应用名称: PDF Merge and Split（PDF 合并与拆分）

开发者: Vladimir Gavranic

适用版本: 1.0.0 及以上

数据收集与使用

本应用根据您通过系统文件选择器自行选择的 PDF 文件，生成新的 PDF 文件（合并、提取页面、删除页面、旋转页面或拆分）。文件仅在您的设备上处理，本应用不会上传、发送或共享。本应用不联网，没有账户，不使用统计分析或广告，不使用相机。我们不会收集、传输或与任何人共享您的数据。

本应用不保留您文件的副本，退出应用后所选文件即被遗忘。应用仅在设备上记录您已接受本隐私政策。本应用已关闭云备份。

权限说明

本应用不申请任何权限，包括网络权限、存储权限和相机权限。文件通过 Android 系统文件选择器打开；新生成的文件会写入手机的 Download/PDFMergeSplit 文件夹。原文件不会被修改。

分享

如果您点击“Share”（分享），文件将交给您自己选择的应用（例如电子邮件或即时通讯），去向完全由您决定，开发者不会收到任何内容。

数据主体权利

您保存的文件位于手机的 Download/PDFMergeSplit 文件夹中，您可以随时自行删除。卸载应用将删除应用设置；已保存的文件会保留，直到您自行删除。

免责声明

本应用以整页为单位处理 PDF，不编辑页面中的文字，也无法打开或移除密码。原文件中的表单、书签和链接在新文件中可能会丢失。发送或删除原文件之前，请务必先打开并检查结果。

联系方式

如果您对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请通过 gavranic76@gmail.com 联系我们。

PDF Merge and Split - Privacy Policy (English)

App Information

App name: PDF Merge and Split

Developer: Vladimir Gavranic

Applies to: version 1.0.0 and later

Data Collection and Usage

The app makes new PDF files (merge, extract pages, delete pages, rotate pages or split) from the PDF files that you choose yourself with the Android system file picker. The files are processed only on your device; the app does not upload, send or share them. The app does not use the internet and has no accounts, analytics or advertising, and it does not use the camera. We do not collect, transmit or share your data with anyone.

The app does not keep copies of your files: after you leave the app the chosen files are forgotten. It only stores on the device that you accepted this Privacy Policy. Cloud backup is switched off for this app.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions, including no network, storage or camera permission. Files are opened through the Android system file picker, and the new files are written into your phone's Download/PDFMergeSplit folder. The original files are never changed.

Sharing

If you tap "Share", the file is handed to the app you choose (for example e-mail or a messenger). Where it goes is entirely your decision; the developer receives nothing.

Your Rights

The files you saved are in the Download/PDFMergeSplit folder on your phone and you can delete them yourself at any time. Uninstalling the app removes its settings; the saved files stay until you delete them.

Disclaimer

The app works with whole pages: it does not edit the text inside a page and it cannot open or remove passwords. Forms, bookmarks and links of the original files can be lost in the new file. Always open the result and check it before you send it or delete the original.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at gavranic76@gmail.com.
